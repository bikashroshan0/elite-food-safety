Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about ISO standards and validity.

What is the validity of an ISO certificate? +
An ISO certificate is typically valid for 3 years, provided the organization successfully passes mandatory annual surveillance audits.
What is a Surveillance Audit? +
A surveillance audit is conducted every 12 months to verify that your organization is maintaining the management system and continuing to comply with ISO standards. It is mandatory to maintain the certificate.
When is recertification required? +
Recertification is required every 3 years. A full recertification audit is conducted before the expiry of the current certificate to renew it for another 3-year cycle.
Which ISO certification is right for my business? +
It depends on your industry and goals. ISO 9001 (QMS) is universal for quality. If you handle food, ISO 22000 is ideal. For IT, ISO 27001 is standard. Our consultants can help you identify the best framework for your specific operational needs.
