Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our laboratory testing services.

Are your testing reports accepted by FSSAI and other regulators? +
Yes. We work exclusively with NABL-accredited and FSSAI-notified laboratory partners. Our reports are legally valid and widely accepted by government regulatory bodies for compliance and licensing.
What is the Turnaround Time (TAT) for a standard test? +
The TAT depends entirely on the type of test. Chemical analyses can often be completed in 2-4 days, while certain microbiological tests (like TPC or Salmonella) require specific incubation periods that take 5-7 days.
Do you test for Nutritional Labeling? +
Absolutely. We provide comprehensive nutritional analysis required for food packaging labels, including calories, proteins, fats, carbohydrates, sugars, sodium, and specific vitamin/mineral profiling.
What are swab tests and why do I need them? +
Swab tests involve taking samples from food preparation surfaces, equipment, or workers' hands. They are crucial for verifying that your cleaning and sanitation processes are actually effectively killing harmful bacteria.
