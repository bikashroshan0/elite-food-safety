Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our specialized support and documentation services.

What is a PMEGP Project Report and why do I need it? +
The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) provides financial assistance and subsidies to set up new micro-enterprises. A highly detailed, professionally drafted Project Report is mandatory for banks to approve your loan under this scheme.
Are Medical Fitness Certificates really mandatory for my staff? +
Yes. Under FSSAI regulations, any employee directly handling food must undergo an annual medical examination. They must possess a valid Medical Fitness Certificate in the prescribed FSSAI format to prove they are free from communicable diseases.
What does Packaging Compliance entail? +
It ensures your food packaging meets Legal Metrology rules and FSSAI labeling guidelines. We check that mandatory details (like nutritional info, allergens, veg/non-veg logos, and manufacturer details) are correctly placed and formatted before you print your packaging.
How can the Internship Programs benefit students? +
Our internship programs provide students with hands-on exposure to real-world industrial compliance, auditing processes, and FSSAI regulations. It bridges the gap between academic theory and practical industry requirements.
