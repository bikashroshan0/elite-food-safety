Understanding FSSAI

Good food deserves great standards.

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) mandates that every Food Business Operator (FBO) must obtain a 14-digit registration or license number. This is not just a legal requirement—it is a mark of quality that builds consumer trust.

Whether you are launching a home bakery, managing a bustling restaurant, or exporting manufactured goods, maintaining active FSSAI compliance protects your brand from penalties and operational halts.

Why is it mandatory?

  • Legal prerequisite to commence any food business.
  • Builds consumer trust and brand credibility.
  • Prevents heavy penalties and operational shutdowns.
  • Essential for corporate tie-ups and online delivery platforms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about FSSAI registration.

What is the validity of an FSSAI License? +
An FSSAI license can be issued for a period ranging from 1 to 5 years, depending on your preference and the fee paid during registration. It must be renewed before expiration.
Do home bakers need an FSSAI registration? +
Yes. Irrespective of the scale, anyone manufacturing, selling, or handling food, including home bakers, must have at least a Basic FSSAI Registration.
What happens if I don't renew my license on time? +
Failure to renew your FSSAI license before its expiry incurs a penalty of ₹100 per day. If not renewed within a specific grace period, the license is canceled, requiring a fresh application.
How long does it take to get the FSSAI certificate? +
Basic Registration usually takes 7-10 working days. State and Central Licenses can take between 30 to 45 days, subject to document verification and department queries.
Can I upgrade my Basic Registration to a State License? +
Yes, if your annual turnover crosses ₹12 Lakhs, you are legally required to apply for a modification/upgrade from a Basic Registration to a State License.
