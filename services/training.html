Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our food safety training programs.

Is FoSTaC training legally mandatory? +
Yes. FSSAI mandates that all licensed Food Business Operators (FBOs) must have at least one trained and certified Food Safety Supervisor for every 25 food handlers or part thereof on all their premises.
Do you conduct training on-site at our facility? +
Yes, we offer highly flexible delivery methods. We can conduct in-person training at your facility, host sessions at our training centers, or provide comprehensive online webinars based on your preference.
Are your certificates recognized by the government? +
Absolutely. Our FoSTaC training programs are delivered by FSSAI-empowered trainers, and the certificates issued upon completion are fully recognized by government regulatory bodies.
How long do the training sessions usually last? +
The duration varies by course. Basic awareness programs may take 4-6 hours, while advanced modules like HACCP or advanced FoSTaC can span 1 to 2 full days to ensure comprehensive understanding.
Can you customize the training for my specific industry? +
Yes. We specialize in tailoring our training content, case studies, and practical examples to suit specific sectors, whether you run a bakery, a cloud kitchen, a manufacturing plant, or a retail chain.
