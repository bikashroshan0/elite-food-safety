Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our audit and documentation services.

How often should we conduct an internal food safety audit? +
It is recommended to conduct internal audits at least bi-annually (every 6 months) to ensure continuous compliance and to stay prepared for sudden external regulatory inspections.
Why is third-party auditing important? +
A third-party audit provides an unbiased, expert evaluation of your systems. It brings unparalleled credibility to your brand, builds trust with stakeholders, and ensures you aren't missing "blind spots" that internal teams might overlook.
What exactly is a Hygiene Rating Audit? +
It is a certification system initiated by FSSAI to rate food businesses (like restaurants and cafes) on their hygiene and safety standards. Earning an "Excellent" rating visibly boosts customer confidence.
Do you create documentation from scratch? +
Yes. We can design and implement a complete Food Safety Management System from the ground up, including custom Food Safety Manuals, SOPs, Formats, and Traceability plans tailored to your specific facility.
