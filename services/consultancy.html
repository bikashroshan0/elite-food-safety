Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our consultancy and support programs.

Do you help with end-to-end factory setups? +
Yes! Our New Factory Setup Consultancy covers everything from initial plant layout design and machinery selection to final documentation and securing required licenses.
What does your Documentation Support include? +
We prepare customized SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), compliance policies, operational manuals, and record formats essential for maintaining a Food Safety Management System.
How can a Mock Audit help my business? +
A Mock Audit is a pre-assessment of your facility and processes that mimics a real external audit. It helps identify critical gaps so you can fix them before the actual regulatory inspectors arrive, ensuring you pass with flying colors.
Do you provide specific consultancy for Water Plants? +
Yes, we offer specialized consultancy for Packaged Drinking Water and Mineral Water plants, assisting with plant design, BIS compliance, FSSAI regulations, and overall documentation.
What is CAPA support? +
CAPA stands for Corrective and Preventive Action. When non-conformities are found in your processes or during an audit, we help you identify the root cause and implement strategies to fix the issue and prevent it from happening again.
