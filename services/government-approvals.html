Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding government registrations and NOCs.

What is the difference between CTE and CTO? +
CTE (Consent to Establish) is required before you begin construction or set up a factory. CTO (Consent to Operate) is required after setup, before you actually start production or operations. Both are issued by the State Pollution Control Board.
Is MSME / Udyam Registration mandatory? +
While not strictly mandatory for operations, MSME registration is highly recommended. It provides legal protection against delayed payments, offers subsidized bank loans, and makes you eligible for various government tenders and schemes.
How long does it take to get a Fire NOC? +
The timeline varies based on your state and the size of your facility. Typically, after the application and physical inspection by the fire department, it takes between 3 to 4 weeks to receive the final Fire NOC.
Do you help with license renewals? +
Yes. We provide continuous support. We track the validity of your Trade Licenses, Factory Licenses, and NOCs, and assist with the complete renewal process before they expire to prevent penalties.
